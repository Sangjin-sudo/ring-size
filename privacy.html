Privacy policy

Last updated 13 October 2026. Published by Quillbay Labs.

The short version: True Ring Size does not collect, send or sell any personal information.

What stays on your device

Your screen calibration, your last measurement and your chosen size system are saved in your browser's storage on your own device so the app remembers them next time. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Clearing the app's or site's data removes them.

What we do not do

  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking code.
  • No access to your camera, photos, contacts or location.

Hosting

The app's files are delivered by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address when your device downloads the files and may keep it in its server logs under GitHub's privacy statement. We do not have access to those logs.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, for example if advertising is added later, this page will be updated before the change takes effect and the date above will change.
