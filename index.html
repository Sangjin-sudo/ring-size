Lay the ring flat on the circle. Drag on the circle or use the slider until the dark circle just fills the inside of the ring, with no dark edge hidden under the metal.

  1. Wrap a thin strip of paper around the base of the finger. Snug, but it should still slide over the knuckle.
  2. Mark where the strip overlaps, then lay it flat on the line below with one end at the left edge.
  3. Drag the marker to your mark, or type the length if you used a ruler.

Getting it right
  • Measure at the end of the day, when fingers are warm. Cold fingers are smaller.
  • If the knuckle is much wider than the base, measure both and choose a size between them.
  • Wide bands (6 mm and over) fit tighter: go up half a US size.
  • Measuring twice and getting the same answer is the best check there is.
  • A paper strip stretches and slips. A ring that already fits is the more reliable method.
